# Ninja Adventure Asset Pack

- Source: https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack
- License: CC0 1.0. See `LICENSE.txt` for the pack license text.
- Author: Pixel-boy and AAA
- Imported scope: scene props, landmarks, floor terrain transitions, interior
  floor, small FX, the 16px resource/tool icon family used by the HUD and
  inventory, and construction-card icons cropped from the house, bed, fire,
  and furniture atlases. Construction cards use the source regions documented
  in `scripts/ninja_adventure_assets.gd` and are rendered with nearest-neighbor
  filtering.
- Character artwork is intentionally not imported; the existing protagonist artwork remains in `assets/player_character_sheet.png`.
